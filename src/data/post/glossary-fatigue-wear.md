**Fatigue Wear** is the loss of knife material by sub-surface crack propagation under repeated thermal-mechanical cycling. Each cut cycle heats the edge, then cools it; the temperature swing + the mechanical stress grows micro-cracks. Eventually a chip breaks off.

## Where fatigue wear shows up

- Tissue slitter > 1,000 m/min: the edge cycles 30+ °C per second
- Film slitter > 600 m/min: similar, with thermal fatigue micro-cracking at the hone
- Aluminium foil slitter: high-frequency thermal cycling
- Any high-speed dry cut on a tough substrate

## Fixes

- Apply a hot-hardness coating (TiAlN, AlCrN)
- Switch to a hot-hardness steel (M35, M42 HSS)
- Add active cooling (air or water-mist) aimed at the cut zone
- Reduce line speed within the knife's rated window
