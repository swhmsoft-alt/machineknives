**undefined** Titanium Nitride (TiN) is a PVD (Physical Vapour Deposition) ceramic coating applied to a precision blade in a vacuum chamber at 400–500 °C. The gold-coloured TiN layer (2–4 µm) reduces coefficient of friction and increases surface hardness, extending knife life 20–30 % on most substrates.

## When to use

- Paper and film slitting (general purpose)
- Metal shear blades (carbon steel ≤ 6 mm)
- Granulator bed knives on light-duty applications
- Anywhere a 20–30 % life gain justifies the 8–15 % cost premium

## When NOT to use

- Above 600 °C edge temperature (degrades, use TiAlN or AlCrN instead)
- On substrates that need ultra-low friction (use DLC)
- On very thin knives where the 3 µm coating can dominate the edge profile
- On food-contact cutting where TiN's gold colour is rejected (use CrN)
