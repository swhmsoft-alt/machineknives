AISI 440A Martensitic Stainless Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 0.60–0.75 %, Cr 16.0–18.0 %, Mo ≤ 0.75 %, Mn ≤ 1.00 %, Si ≤ 1.00 %, Ni ≤ 0.75 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 215–240; hardened HRC 54–58

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 1,010–1,050 °C, oil or gas quench, temper 150–200 °C

**Properties:** The lower-carbon member of the 440 family. Less hardenable than 440C but tougher. Better corrosion resistance than 420.

**Applications:** Food slicer blades, meat processing, medical instruments where impact resistance matters.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ JIS SUS440A, DIN 1.4110, GB 7Cr17.
