**undefined** Titanium Carbonitride (TiCN) is a PVD ceramic coating, harder and more wear-resistant than TiN, with a blue-grey colour. Applied at 400–500 °C, 2–4 µm thick. The carbon content in the coating structure gives TiCN a higher hardness (2,800 HV vs 2,300 HV) and lower friction than TiN.

## When to use

- Abrasive non-woven slitting
- Recycled polymer granulator bed knives
- Glass-filled polymer cutting
- Coated paper slitting
- Corrugated slitting

## When NOT to use

- Above 400 °C edge temperature (carbon in the coating oxidises, loses hardness)
- Stainless or aluminium (DLC is better)
- High-temperature cutting (TiAlN, AlCrN better)
