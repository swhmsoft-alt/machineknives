GB YG10 Tungsten Carbide (K30) is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** WC 90 %, Co 10 %, no grain-growth inhibitors, WC grain 1.5–2.0 µm

**Hardness:** HRA 89.0–90.5 (≈ HRC 76–78)

**Heat treatment:** Sintered at 1,380–1,460 °C, no further heat treatment

**Properties:** Standard medium-Co YG10. Higher toughness than YG6 with only slightly lower hardness.

**Applications:** Medium-impact granulator rotors, shear inserts, wear parts requiring moderate impact resistance.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ ISO K30, no precise Western equivalent.
