17-4 PH (UNS S17400, AISI 630, DIN 1.4542) is a precipitation-hardening martensitic stainless steel combining high strength (1,300 MPa UTS achievable) with good corrosion resistance. It is specified for high-strength structural blades and die-casting dies where standard 400-series stainless cannot deliver the load-bearing capacity.



**Standard composition:** C ≤ 0.07 %, Cr 15.0–17.5 %, Ni 3.0–5.0 %, Cu 3.0–5.0 %, Nb 0.15–0.45 %, Mn ≤ 1.00 %, Si ≤ 1.00 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Solution annealed HB 320; aged to HRC 40–44 (H900), 36–40 (H1025), 28–32 (H1150)

**Heat treatment:** Solution anneal 1,040 °C, age at 480–620 °C for 1–4 h depending on target hardness

**Properties:** A precipitation-hardening stainless. Achieves high strength (1,300 MPa UTS) and good corrosion resistance. The "H900" condition gives the highest hardness; "H1150" gives the best toughness. Used for high-strength structural blades and dies.

**Applications:** Aerospace structural blades, die-casting dies, plastic-injection moulds, food-processing parts requiring high strength + corrosion resistance.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.4542, GB 05Cr17Ni4Cu4Nb, AISI 630.
