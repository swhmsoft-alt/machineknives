AISI A6 Air-Hardening Cold-Work Tool Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 0.65–0.75 %, Cr 0.90–1.30 %, Mo 0.90–1.40 %, Mn 1.80–2.50 %, Si 0.20–0.40 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 217–248; hardened HRC 54–60

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 830–870 °C, air cool, double temper 175–540 °C

**Properties:** High toughness from the lower carbon and higher Mn. Wear resistance lower than D2 (smaller carbide fraction). Deep-hardening with low distortion. Used for impact-loaded parts where D2 would chip.

**Applications:** Granulator rotor knives, shear blades on thick plate, crusher blades, blanking dies for thick mild steel, coining dies.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.2343 (close).
