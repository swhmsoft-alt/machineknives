GB YG6 Tungsten Carbide (K20) is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** WC 94 %, Co 6 %, no grain-growth inhibitors, WC grain 1.5–2.5 µm

**Hardness:** HRA 91.0–92.0 (≈ HRC 78–80)

**Heat treatment:** Sintered at 1,400–1,480 °C, no further heat treatment

**Properties:** Standard general-purpose coarse-grain YG6. Lower cost than YG6X. Lower wear resistance but higher impact resistance.

**Applications:** Non-precision wear parts, granulator bed knives (light duty), general mechanical wear parts.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ ISO K20, no precise Western equivalent.
