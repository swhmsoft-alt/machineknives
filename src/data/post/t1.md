AISI T1 Tungsten High-Speed Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 0.65–0.80 %, Cr 3.75–4.50 %, W 17.25–18.75 %, V 0.90–1.30 %, Mo ≤ 0.10 %, Co ≤ 0.10 %, Mn ≤ 0.40 %, Si ≤ 0.50 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 217–248; hardened HRC 62–65

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 1,260–1,280 °C, oil or salt quench, double temper 540–565 °C

**Properties:** Classic tungsten HSS. Excellent hot hardness from the 18 % W. Lower Mo than M-series, which makes it tougher. Now largely replaced by M-series for cost reasons, but still used in some hot-work applications.

**Applications:** Legacy cutting tools, high-temperature slitter blades (pre-M42 era), die-casting punches.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ JIS SKH2, DIN 1.3355, GB W18Cr4V, BS BT1.
