**Surface Roughness** is the arithmetic average of the absolute deviations of the surface profile from the mean, expressed in micrometres (µm) and denoted Ra. A lower Ra means a smoother surface.

## Typical Ra by application

- Precision slitter (film, foil): Ra ≤ 0.2 µm
- Standard slitter (paper): Ra ≤ 0.4 µm
- Tissue slitter: Ra ≤ 0.4 µm
- Granulator: Ra ≤ 0.8 µm (less critical)
- Plate shear: Ra ≤ 0.4 µm on the cutting face

## How to measure

- Contact profilometer: most accurate, trace over 4–12 mm
- Optical profilometer: non-contact, suitable for soft coatings
- Stylus profilometer: classic, contact-based
- Compare to a calibrated roughness specimen for spot checks
