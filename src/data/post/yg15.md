GB YG15 Tungsten Carbide (K30–K40) is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** WC 85 %, Co 15 %, no grain-growth inhibitors, WC grain 1.5–2.5 µm

**Hardness:** HRA 87.5–89.0 (≈ HRC 74–76)

**Heat treatment:** Sintered at 1,360–1,440 °C, no further heat treatment

**Properties:** High-Co carbide for the toughest impact applications. Lower wear life than YG6X but survives blows that would shatter finer grades.

**Applications:** Recycling rotors, automotive shredder residue (ASR) granulators, primary shredders, hammer-mill inserts.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ ISO K30–K40.
