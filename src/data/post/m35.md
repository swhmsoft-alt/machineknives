AISI M35 (UNS T11335) is a 5 % Co modification of M2 — the cobalt boosts hot hardness from HRC 56 (M2 at 600 °C) to HRC 60 (M35 at 600 °C), extending edge life in high-temperature cutting. Hardened to HRC 63–66, it is used for high-speed slitter blades (>800 m/min on tissue / film) and dry cutting applications where M2 hot hardness is marginal.



**Standard composition:** C 0.80–0.90 %, Cr 3.75–4.50 %, Mo 4.50–5.50 %, W 5.50–6.75 %, V 1.75–2.25 %, Co 4.50–5.50 %, Mn ≤ 0.40 %, Si ≤ 0.50 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 235–269; hardened HRC 63–66

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 1,200–1,225 °C, oil or salt quench, double temper 540–565 °C

**Properties:** The 5 % Co addition boosts hot hardness to ~ HRC 60 at 600 °C (vs M2 HRC 56). Better edge retention at high temperature. Toughness slightly lower than M2.

**Applications:** High-speed slitter blades (>800 m/min on tissue or film), foil slitting, dry cutting applications.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ JIS SKD55, DIN 1.3243, GB W6Mo5Cr4V2Co5, BS BM35.
