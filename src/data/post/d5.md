AISI D5 (UNS T30405, DIN 1.2601) is a cobalt- and molybdenum-modified D2 (2.5–3.5 % Co, 0.7–1.2 % Mo added). The cobalt improves through-hardening of heavy sections (>50 mm) while retaining wear resistance similar to D2. Used for heavy-section shear blades, large granulator rotors and rock-crusher blades.



**Standard composition:** C 1.40–1.60 %, Cr 11.0–13.0 %, Mo 0.70–1.20 %, V 0.40–1.00 %, Co 2.50–3.50 %, Mn ≤ 0.60 %, Si ≤ 0.60 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 220–255; hardened HRC 58–62

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 980–1,020 °C, air cool, double temper 510–540 °C

**Properties:** Slightly better through-hardening than D2 from the Co and Mo additions. Wear resistance similar to D2. Used for heavy-section blades that need full hardness through 50+ mm sections.

**Applications:** Heavy-section shear blades, large granulator knives, rock-crusher blades, draw dies for thick plate.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.2601 (close).
