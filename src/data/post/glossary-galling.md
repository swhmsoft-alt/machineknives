**undefined** is severe adhesive wear where the substrate material welds to the cutting edge, tears off, and re-welds in a continuous cycle. The cut surface shows deep gouges, the edge is built up with substrate material, and the line speed must be dropped to keep cut quality in spec.

## Substrates prone to galling

- Austenitic stainless (304, 316, 321): nickel catalysis
- Aluminium (5052, 6061, 7075): low melting point
- Copper, brass: low hardness + high thermal conductivity
- Gummy plastics: high friction at the cut
- Titanium (rare in industrial cutting, but severe)

## Fixes

- Apply a low-friction coating (DLC for stainless and aluminium, ta-C for aluminium)
- Use high-pressure flood coolant (50+ bar at the nozzle)
- Drop the line speed to reduce heat generation
- Switch to a hot-hardness steel (M2 HSS, M42) with a more positive clearance angle
