GB 6CrW2Si (China GB/T 1299 standard) is a tungsten-modified 5 Cr-type hot-work tool steel — used primarily for hot shear blades, punches and short-run hot-work dies. Slightly higher hot hardness than the more common 5CrNiMo / H13 family due to the W addition, with comparable toughness.



**Standard composition:** C 0.55–0.65 %, Cr 1.00–1.30 %, W 2.00–2.50 %, Si 0.50–0.80 %, Mn ≤ 0.40 %, V ≤ 0.30 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 217–270; hardened HRC 53–58

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 850–900 °C, oil cool, double temper 200–250 °C

**Properties:** A Chinese-standard W-bearing tool steel. The 2.5 % W gives good hot hardness and wear resistance. Tough and resistant to thermal shock.

**Applications:** Hot shear blades, wood-cutting saws, leather and rubber cutting knives, paper slitter blades for tough applications.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.2550 (similar), no precise Western equivalent.
