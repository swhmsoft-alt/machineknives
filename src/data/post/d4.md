AISI D4 High-Carbon, High-Chromium + Tungsten Cold-Work Tool Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 2.05–2.40 %, Cr 11.0–13.0 %, Mo 0.70–1.20 %, V 0.15–1.00 %, W 0.50–0.80 %, Mn ≤ 0.60 %, Si ≤ 0.60 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 217–248; hardened HRC 58–64

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 970–1,010 °C, air or oil cool, double temper 150–200 °C

**Properties:** Slightly better hot hardness than D3 from the W addition. Wear resistance and toughness similar to D3.

**Applications:** Slitter blades on highly abrasive substrates; cold-heading dies; blanking and piercing dies for thick stainless plate.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.2436, GB Cr12W.
