AISI 440B Martensitic Stainless Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 0.75–0.95 %, Cr 16.0–18.0 %, Mo ≤ 0.75 %, Mn ≤ 1.00 %, Si ≤ 1.00 %, Ni ≤ 0.75 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 220–250; hardened HRC 56–59

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 1,010–1,050 °C, oil or gas quench, temper 150–200 °C

**Properties:** The middle member of the 440 family. Slightly higher hardness than 440A, similar corrosion resistance.

**Applications:** Industrial cutting tools, bearings, valve components, surgical instruments.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ JIS SUS440B, DIN 1.4112, GB 8Cr17.
