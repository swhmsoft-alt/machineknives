**undefined** is the total indicated reading (TIR) of a circular knife as it rotates on a precision spindle. Runout is the sum of the knife's out-of-roundness, its concentricity on the spindle, and the spindle's own error.

## Typical runout by application

- Precision slitter (film, foil): ≤ 0.005 mm TIR
- Standard slitter (paper, film): ≤ 0.010 mm TIR
- Tissue slitter: ≤ 0.015 mm TIR
- Granulator rotor: ≤ 0.025 mm TIR (less critical, impact dominates)
- Plate shear: not applicable (single pass)

## How to measure

- Mount the knife on a precision spindle
- Set a dial indicator on the OD or on a reference face
- Rotate 360°
- Read the TIR = max - min
