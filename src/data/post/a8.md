AISI A8 Air-Hardening Cold-Work Tool Steel is a reference entry for industrial cutting tools and blades. The composition, hardness, heat treatment and application guidance are summarised below for engineering reference.

**Standard composition:** C 0.50–0.60 %, Cr 4.75–5.50 %, Mo 1.15–1.65 %, W 1.00–1.50 %, V 0.30–0.50 %, Mn ≤ 0.50 %, Si 0.75–1.10 %, balance Fe

**Hardness:** Annealed HB 217–248; hardened HRC 56–60

**Heat treatment:** Austenitise 980–1,010 °C, air cool, double temper 175–540 °C

**Properties:** Better hot hardness than A6 from the W addition. Toughness similar to A6. Used for hot-work applications that do not need H13-class hot hardness.

**Applications:** Cold-header dies, punches for thick plate, shear blades on tough substrate, hot-piercing punches for billet.

**Cross-reference:** ≈ DIN 1.2343 (similar).
