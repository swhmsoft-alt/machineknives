# Custom Blade – Reverse-Engineered from Sample

If your blade is obsolete, vendor-locked, or simply worn beyond recognition, we can reverse-engineer a replacement from the worn part. Send us the part (or a 3D scan / drawing), tell us what is failing on it, and our engineering team will design a replacement that fits — often with an upgraded material grade or geometry.

## Process

1. **Receive sample / drawing.** Send by courier or as STEP / IGES / DXF.
2. **CMM measurement.** We measure the critical features on the worn part and reconstruct the geometry.
3. **Material recommendation.** Based on the wear pattern and your process data (line speed, material cut, downtime cost), we propose a material grade and hardness.
4. **Drawing approval.** We issue a 2D / 3D drawing for your written sign-off before any steel is ordered.
5. **Manufacture and deliver.** Same QC chain as our standard SKUs — CMM inspection report, hardness, surface records shipped with the part.

## When Reverse-Engineering Helps Most

- The original blade is no longer available from the OEM.
- The OEM lead time is too long for your maintenance window.
- The original blade is failing prematurely — we can usually identify why and propose an upgrade.
- A second-source supplier is needed for risk management.

## Materials We Work In

D2, SKD11, DC53, M2 HSS, M4 HSS, H13, H21, tungsten carbide tipped and solid carbide, plus PVD coatings (TiN / TiCN / CrN / DLC).
