## Material Selection

- **M2 HSS (1.3343)** —first choice for granulators cutting filled, glass-fibre reinforced or abrasive plastics.
- **D2 (1.2379)** —slightly tougher, used where contamination is heavy (recycled feedstock).
- **Tungsten carbide tipped** —for the most abrasive feedstocks, typically 8—0× the life of HSS but with a higher unit cost.

## Stator / Bed Knife

The rotor knife works in shear against a stator (bed knife). We supply matched sets; rotor-to-bed clearance target is 0.1—.3 mm depending on material.
