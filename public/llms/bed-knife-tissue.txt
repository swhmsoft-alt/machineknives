## Material

- D2 (high-carbon, high-chromium cold-work tool steel)
- Hardness target: HRC 60 (within HRC 58-62 typical as-tempered window)
- Secondary hardening near 500 °C tempering
- Elastic modulus: approximately 200 GPa
- Density: approximately 7.7 g/cm³
- Thermal conductivity: approximately 20-25 W/(m·K) (low — contributes to edge-heat accumulation at high web speed)
- Carbide type: M7C3 (chromium-rich, conferring abrasion resistance)

## Process

- Austenitization at 1000-1050 °C, followed by air or oil quench
- Double temper (transformation of retained austenite)
- Vacuum or controlled-atmosphere furnace prevents surface decarburization
- Inter-process datum: post-temper hardness verification (Rockwell C, HRC 60) is mandatory before precision grinding
- Distortion allowance: approximately 0.1-0.3 mm per side reserved in stock
- Edge preparation: 15 μm micro-hone + 18° clearance angle

## Operating Reference

- Reference web speed: 1200 m/min on 4-ply tissue laminate
- Reference edge temperature at that speed: approximately 180 °C
- Burr control target: ≤ 50 µm
- Documented service-life outcome: 11 days → 34 days at the same D2/HRC 60 grade, with edge preparation as the only changed variable

## Failure Mode (Sharp Edge)

- Sharp edge (micro-hone under 5 μm) at 1200 m/min is susceptible to thermal-fatigue micro-cracking at ~180 °C
- Failure mode: sub-millimetre edge chipping, followed by accelerated burr formation
- Resolution: 15 μm micro-hone + 18° clearance angle (no steel change required)
